## Priority Issues

- **[P0] Window content cropped on a scaled display.** At 1.25x display scale, cells render ~37 px instead of 30; the 660 px window shows ~17.6 of 22 rows and clips the side panel (score numbers, key list). Fix: draw through a fixed 560x660 logical camera each frame, or handle display scale explicitly. Command: /impeccable adapt
- **[P1] Nothing helps read where an unfamiliar piece will land.** Fix: faint landing outline; colour by cell count (3, 4, 5). Command: /impeccable shape
- **[P1] No pause, and R destroys a game without warning.** Fix: P to pause and pause on focus loss; R restarts only after game over or on second press; key repeat for held Left/Right. Command: /impeccable harden
- **[P2] Peak moments have no feedback.** Fix: brief flash on cleared rows before collapse; highlight level number on change. Command: /impeccable animate
- **[P2] Side panel has no hierarchy; game over is hard to read.** Fix: small dim labels with large right-aligned numbers, score dominant; dim the well under a game-over panel showing the final score. Command: /impeccable typeset
